**Summary:**

## Market Implications

**BFSI (UK/EU):** 
Supervisory attention has intensified following Parliamentary scrutiny, with banks subject to active enforcement under the PRA's SS1/23 model risk regime. Key friction points remain around senior manager accountability, model risk for advanced and generative AI, and third-party cloud concentration. The clear message is that firms should not wait for bespoke AI regulation: regulators expect AI to be governed now, with documented accountability, risk appetite, explainability and resilience testing already in place.


**Technology / CMT / Retail / FMCG:** 
The most critical compliance deadline for most enterprises remains August 2, 2026, when requirements for Annex III high-risk AI systems were originally set to become enforceable — including AI used in employment, credit decisions, education, and law enforcement contexts
 — now deferred to December 2027, but transparency obligations are unchanged. 
Shadow AI usage creates unquantified EU AI Act exposure: 64% of workers bypass corporate security with personal logins and unauthorised tools. Under the EU AI Act, that isn't just an IT problem — it is regulatory exposure that surfaces in the next audit cycle.


**Pharma / Energy:** 
By August 2026, high-risk AI systems in the financial sector must comply with specific requirements, including AI used for credit scoring and risk assessment for insurance pricing. The Digital Omnibus proposal includes provisions to align AI Act requirements with existing financial sector regulations such as DORA.
 Pharma firms deploying AI in clinical decision-support will face the extended Annex I embedded-product regime (August 2028) but must map current deployments now.

**Africa (all sectors):** 
Startups face growing compliance risks, including fines, mandatory data localisation, and stricter oversight in sectors like finance and healthcare.
 
The trade-off between regulation and innovation is a false dichotomy. Predictable and principled governance frameworks can reduce uncertainty and support more sustainable innovation.
