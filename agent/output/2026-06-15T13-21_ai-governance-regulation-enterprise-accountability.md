## 3. Latest Developments

### 🇪🇺 EU AI Omnibus — High-Risk Deadline Extended to December 2027

On 7 May 2026, the Council of the EU announced a provisional political agreement with the European Parliament on the "AI Omnibus," part of the EU's Digital Omnibus simplification agenda, to streamline parts of the EU AI Act ahead of key compliance deadlines.
 
High-risk obligations under Annex III now apply from 2 December 2027, and AI embedded in regulated products under Annex I applies from 2 August 2028.
 
The agreement also introduces new prohibitions on AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery and child sexual abuse material, reduces regulatory duplication for industrial AI products, and introduces tailored accommodations for SMEs.
