## Decision

The following architecture is the **governing operating model** for TheAIReadyist.

| Layer | Concept | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Mission** | **AI Readiness** | The promise / market front door |
| **Category** | **Decision Performance** | What the platform owns, measures and sells |
| **Proprietary Engine** | **Constraint Intelligence** | The moat — surfaced as signature methodology, not sold as the category |
| **Discipline** | **Decision Intelligence** | Retained as an internal discipline and methodology; **not** the primary market-facing category |

**Products** (sit beneath the Decision Performance category):

- Decision Performance Index (DPI)
- Executive Signals
- Assessments (incl. DORA / NIS2 / EU AI Act — run as funnel, not as category)
- Benchmarks *(future)*
- Research *(future)*

### Organising principle

> Become **AI Ready** by improving **Decision Performance** through **Decision Intelligence**, using **Constraint Intelligence** to find the one limit that matters most.

### One-line model

**Mission: AI Readiness · Category: Decision Performance · Engine: Constraint Intelligence · Discipline: Decision Intelligence · Imperative: credible DPI volume.**

---

## Mandate

1. **All future build packages must reference DR-001.**
2. **No future platform work proceeds without demonstrating alignment to this architecture.**
3. **Alignment test** — every proposed asset, feature, page or assessment must answer *yes* to:
   - Does it improve Decision Performance, or apply the discipline (Decision Intelligence) or engine (Constraint Intelligence) that does?
   - Does it drive, or depend on, **credible DPI volume**?
   - Does it respect the non-negotiables (pillars not renamed; six-pillar model intact; DPI unaltered; no new assessment without explicit approval; no category competing with Decision Performance)?
   If any answer is *no*, the work does not proceed under this operating model.

## Explicitly out of scope under DR-001 (current phase)

- Building Constraint Intelligence before evidence justifies it (see the Constraint Intelligence Readiness Report).
- New strategy packages or further theoretical expansion of WP12.
- New assessments, platform redesigns, or feature expansion for its own sake.

The current phase is **operational learning, not feature expansion.** Success = the platform begins accumulating proprietary Decision Performance data.
