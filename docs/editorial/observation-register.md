# Observation Register

**Canonical register** for the operational period (Editorial Operating Model v1, D4).
**Supersedes:** `docs/observation/emerging-pattern-register.md` (fields aligned + `Editorial Notes` added).
**Rule:** capture **observations, not constraints.** No naming, clustering or taxonomy until the 60-day evidence gate (D6).

---

## How to use
- One row per distinct recurring/notable pattern. Log liberally.
- **Observation ID:** `OBS-001`, sequential, never reused.
- **Confidence:** `Low` (2–3×, anecdotal) · `Medium` (recurring across weeks/sources) · `High` (frequent, multi-source, consistent) — evidence strength only.
- **Associated Pillar:** tentative; may be wrong.
- Never delete a row — supersede with a note in Editorial Notes.
- Do **not** call anything a "constraint."

## Handoff to the 60-day gate
At day 60 these rows feed Q1–Q3 of the Constraint Intelligence Readiness Report:
- Q1 (patterns exist?) ← register contents
- Q2 (cluster?) ← grouping of rows
- Q3 (taxonomy evidence-backed?) ← do clusters map to candidate categories

A pattern becomes a candidate *constraint* only after clearing the gate.
