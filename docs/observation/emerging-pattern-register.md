# Emerging Pattern Register

**Aligns to:** DR-001 · Precursor to (not yet) Constraint Intelligence
**Purpose:** capture recurring observations during the observation period as **evidence**.

> **These are patterns, NOT constraints.** Do not name, formalise, or build a taxonomy from them yet. The register is raw evidence; the Constraint Intelligence Readiness Report later decides whether genuine constraint categories have emerged. Capture liberally; classify nothing.

---

## How to use

- Add a row whenever a recurring observation appears across signals (the observation dashboard's tags/recent list and the weekly report surface candidates).
- **Pattern ID:** `EP-001`, `EP-002`, … (sequential; never reused).
- **Confidence:** `Low` (seen 2–3×, anecdotal) · `Medium` (recurring across weeks/sources) · `High` (frequent, multi-source, consistent) — evidence strength only, not a constraint claim.
- **Potential Pillar Relationship:** which of the six pillars it *seems* to relate to — tentative, may be wrong.
- Keep descriptions observational ("signals repeatedly note X"), not prescriptive.

## Period-close handoff

At 30–60 days, the patterns here feed **Q1–Q3 of the Constraint Intelligence Readiness Report**:
- Q1 (recurring patterns) ← this register's contents
- Q2 (do they cluster?) ← grouping of these rows
- Q3 (taxonomy evidence-backed?) ← do clusters map to the candidate constraint vocabulary

A pattern only becomes a candidate *constraint* after it clears the readiness gate — never automatically.
