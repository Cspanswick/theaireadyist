What is constraining your organisation's Decision Performance?

The Decision Performance Index measures six dimensions of how your organisation makes decisions — quality, velocity, accountability, capability, value, and resilience. It identifies the single dimension most limiting your ability to make high-value decisions, consistently and at pace.

This measures decision performance — not AI maturity. It assesses how your organisation decides, then maps the constraint to the discipline that will move it.
