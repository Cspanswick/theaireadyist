EU AI Act — Risk Tier Reference

Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 · In force Aug 2026
Tier 1
Unacceptable Risk

Systems in this tier are prohibited by law. There is no compliance path — only decommissioning.

What's banned
  • Real-time biometric surveillance in public
  • Social scoring by governments or employers
  • Subliminal or manipulative AI techniques
  • Predictive policing via profiling
  • Emotion recognition in workplaces / schools
Obligations
Cease & decommission No remediation path
€35M
or 7% global turnover
whichever is higher
Tier 2
High Risk

Where most enterprise AI lives. Heaviest obligations — and where most compliance gaps sit.

Triggers include
  • HR / recruitment screening & scoring
  • Credit, lending & insurance decisions
  • Medical devices & clinical support
  • Critical infrastructure (energy, water, transport)
  • Education assessment
  • Law enforcement tools
Mandatory obligations
Conformity assessment Human oversight Audit trail & logging EU database registration Technical documentation Bias & data testing Named accountability owner
€15M
or 3% global turnover
+ market withdrawal orders
Tier 3
Limited Risk

Transparency obligations only. Users must know they are interacting with AI.

Triggers include
  • Customer-facing chatbots & virtual agents
  • AI-generated content (text, image, audio, video)
  • Synthetic media / deepfakes
  • Emotion recognition in customer interactions
Mandatory obligations
Disclose AI identity Label generated content Mark synthetic media
No requirement for
  • Registration or technical documentation
  • Conformity assessment
€7.5M
or 1.5% global turnover
for failure to disclose
Tier 4
Minimal Risk

No mandatory obligations. The majority of AI in use today falls here — but misclassification is the real exposure.

Examples
  • Spam & content filters
  • Recommendation engines (non-consequential)
  • AI in video games
  • Internal productivity tools
  • Predictive maintenance (non-critical)
Mandatory obligations
  • None — voluntary codes of conduct encouraged
Key risk
  • Misclassification — document your reasoning, not just your conclusion
  • Scope creep — a system's tier can change as its outputs expand
No fine
unless misclassified
as minimal risk incorrectly
